About Deshaun

More Than a Trainer. A Coach for Life.

Deshaun Beasley founded DB Wellness & Fitness Training with one mission: to bridge the gap between where you are and where you want to be — physically, mentally, and holistically.

With over 8 years of hands-on coaching experience, Deshaun has worked with clients across all fitness levels — from beginners stepping into the gym for the first time to competitive athletes looking to elevate their performance. His approach blends evidence-based strength programming with genuine personal investment in every client's journey.

Deshaun believes fitness is a lifestyle, not a phase. That's why every plan he builds extends beyond the gym — covering nutrition guidance, recovery, mindset, and long-term sustainability. His academic foundation in Exercise and Sport Physiology means every training decision is grounded in science.

The DB Method

Four Pillars of Total Transformation

01

Strength Foundation

Every program begins with building a strong, injury-resistant base. Compound movements, progressive loading, and smart periodization form the backbone of all training plans.

02

Personalized Programming

No two bodies are the same. Deshaun designs every plan from scratch — factoring in your history, limitations, lifestyle, and goals. Zero cookie-cutter plans.

03

Nutrition & Recovery

Training is only part of the equation. Deshaun integrates smart nutrition principles and recovery protocols — sleep, mobility, and stress management — into every plan.

Long-term results require more than physical effort. Deshaun coaches the mental side — building discipline, resilience, and the self-belief to sustain your transformation.
